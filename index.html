📖 Agent-Based Modeling: Theoretical Formulation

1. Complex Adaptive Systems Paradigm

This simulation applies decentralized Agent-Based Modeling (ABM) to simulate epidemiological dynamics within a spatially heterogeneous urban grid. Macro-level patterns (epidemic curves, safe havens, quarantine collapses) emerge purely from localized agent decision trees.

2. Heterogeneous Agent Micro-Rules

  • Civilian Survivor: Repulsive vector calculation away from zombies; attraction to hospitals when infected; foraging at supply depots; evacuation to fortified sanctuaries.
  • Zombie Hostile: Sensory detection radius; pursuit vector towards nearest human; probabilistic viral inoculation upon contact.
  • Metro Police: Area patrol; response to 911 calls; ballistic engagements maintaining tactical distance; tactical retreats when ammunition depletes.
  • SWAT & Military: Ballistic riot shield coverage; high-rate-of-fire suppression; checkpoint perimeter enforcement.
  • Paramedic / Doctor: Triage algorithms; hospital restocking; administration of experimental vaccines.

3. Emergent Phenomenon: Phase Transitions

Small variations in initial parameters (e.g., number of police officers or density of safe zones) produce non-linear bifurcations: either total epidemic burn-through or complete containment.
